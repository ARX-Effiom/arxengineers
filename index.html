Our Philosophy

Every client deserves to feel like your most important client.

Most structural engineers are transactional. A job comes in, calculations go out. ARX is different.

We believe the homeowner who's anxious about their loft conversion deserves the same care and clarity as the architect who sends us ten jobs a year. No one should feel like a number.

That's not a marketing line. It's how we operate every single day — because excellence isn't a service tier, it's a decision.

Anticipate, don't just react.
We flag potential issues before they become your problem. You shouldn't need to know the right questions to ask — we ask them for you.
Plain English, always.
Structural engineering has enough jargon. We explain what we're doing, why it matters, and what it means for your project. No mystification.
Reachable and responsive.
Every enquiry acknowledged the same day. Every question answered properly. We understand your project matters to you — so it matters to us.
No is never just no.
If something can't be done the way you've envisioned it, we tell you why and we find an alternative. There's always a way forward.

What We
Design

Structural engineering for residential and commercial projects across Bristol and the South West.

01
Loft Conversions
Hip-to-gable, dormer, mansard. Ridge beams, floor joists, staircase trimmers, masonry stability — the full structural package for Building Control.
02
Extensions
Single and two-storey rear extensions. Flat roof joists, opening beams, foundations, and full stability design. Bi-fold and sliding door frames handled too.
03
Internal Alterations
Wall removals, new steel frames, lintel design, masonry bearings. We make open-plan possible — safely, efficiently, and with drawings your builder can actually follow.
04
New Builds
Additional storeys, outbuildings, full new-build structural packages. Director-led from concept to Building Control submission.
05
Garage Conversions
Structural design for garage-to-habitable-space conversions. New openings, roof loads, foundation checks — everything Building Control needs.
06
Building Regulations Packages
Full calculation packages for Building Control. Structural drawings, key details, and specifications — clear, compliant, and ready to submit.
